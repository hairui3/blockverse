How It Started

From Passion to Purpose

BLOCKVERSE was born in 2019 from a simple idea: what if building blocks could be more than just toys? Our founders, lifelong collectors and designers, saw an opportunity to create something extraordinary.

Starting in a small garage in Los Angeles, we spent countless hours perfecting our designs, sourcing the highest quality materials, and building a community of like-minded enthusiasts.

Today, we're proud to serve over 50,000 builders across the United States, offering exclusive designs that you won't find anywhere else.
